## Performance Characteristics

Latency Targets (4.35 MB SWA page):
- H2D transfer: 30-50 µs (design target)
- D2H transfer: 30-50 µs (design target)
- Event poll: < 1 µs (O(1) GPU operation)
- Full e2e (submit → poll → complete): 100-150 µs

Throughput:
- Single stream (PCIe 4.0): ~87 GB/s (90% bandwidth)
- 4 concurrent streams: 250-300 GB/s (70% efficiency)
